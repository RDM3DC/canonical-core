## Canon Stability Commitment

Once a paper reaches **v1.0**, its core notation and definitions will not change without a major version bump (v2.0). This ensures:

- **Stable references** – Other work can cite specific versions
- **Clear evolution** – Extensions are additive, not breaking
- **Trust** – The canon is reliable
